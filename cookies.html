Cookie Policy

Current demo configuration: This website is intentionally built without analytics, advertising trackers, marketing pixels, third-party embeds or non-essential cookies. Therefore there is no non-essential cookie consent banner to accept on this version.

Effective date: 2 October 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by a browser. Similar technologies can also include local storage and tracking pixels.

2. What this demo uses

The demo does not set its own cookies and does not use analytics or advertising tracking. The browser or hosting infrastructure may still process technical information such as requests and IP addresses according to the actual hosting provider's configuration. Confirm this before publishing.

3. Analytics

No analytics service is installed. If analytics is added later, update this policy and implement an appropriate consent/control mechanism based on the actual technology, purposes and applicable law before collecting analytics data.

4. Third-party embeds

No Google Maps, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, booking widgets, chat widgets or other third-party embeds are included. These can cause third-party requests and may introduce additional cookies or personal-data processing.

5. Cookie choices

If the production site introduces non-essential cookies, provide a clear choice before those technologies activate where required, avoid pre-ticked optional consent, and provide a way to change or withdraw the choice.

6. Important implementation rule

Do not add an analytics script while leaving this policy unchanged. The cookie banner and policy must match the actual scripts, SDKs, embeds and hosting configuration.
