# Aster & Form Studio — Demo Interior Design Website

This is a static, test-only website template.

## Safety/compliance design choices
- No analytics installed.
- No advertising/marketing pixels.
- No third-party embeds.
- No external stock images.
- No fake reviews/testimonials.
- No unsupported awards, ratings, client counts or performance claims.
- Contact form collects only name, email and optional project description.
- Form consent checkbox is required.
- Demo form does not transmit or store data.
- Privacy, Terms, Cookie and Refund pages included.
- Accessibility basics: semantic landmarks, skip link, labels, keyboard-visible focus, descriptive image alternatives, reduced-motion support and responsive layout.
- Visuals are CSS-generated, so there are no stock-image copyright licenses to maintain.

## Before publishing
1. Replace fictional business name/details.
2. Replace all concept visuals with owned/licensed/authorised portfolio assets.
3. Connect the form only to a reviewed, secure backend.
4. Update the privacy notice to match actual collection, purposes, processors, retention and rights.
5. If analytics/cookies/embeds are added, update the cookie policy and consent implementation.
6. Review the DPDP Act/rules commencement and obligations applicable at launch.
7. Have a qualified Indian lawyer review the legal pages for the real business and service model.
8. Test keyboard navigation, screen reader labels, contrast, mobile layout, form errors, HTTPS, security headers and real hosting logs.
